From Data to Decisions: Improving Clinical Insight Through Pediatric care

Posted by:

Anthony Martina Medical researcher

Projects description

This project explores how pediatric clinical datasets van be translated into clearer, more actionable insights for treatments decisions. The current phase focuses on identifying patterns in longitudinal patient data to improve diagnostic precision and reduce variability in care pathways. The aim is to bridge analytical findings with everyday clinical decision-making.

Stage of project:
Exploratory phase
Methodology
Mixed-methods, interviews + exploratory analysis

What I'm looking for

I'm interested in connecting with researchers or professionals who have experience in adolescent health, preventive care, or related social and policy context. Open to exchanging insight or exploring potential collaboration.
